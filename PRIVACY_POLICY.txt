------------------------------------------------------------
1. INTRODUCTION
------------------------------------------------------------

This Privacy Policy explains how Push Out handles information when you 
play the Game on your mobile device.

By downloading or playing the Game, you acknowledge that you have read
this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use
the Game.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT OURSELVES
------------------------------------------------------------

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your
name, email address, phone number, or any other personal details in
order to play.

We do not operate our own servers that collect or store your personal
information.

The only data the Game creates is your game progress, such as which
levels you have completed or unlocked. This is saved locally on your
device, in the Game's private storage. It is never sent to us. If you
uninstall the Game or clear its data, this progress is deleted.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES (ADVERTISING)
------------------------------------------------------------

The Game shows advertisements using Google AdMob, a service provided by
Google LLC. The Game displays two kinds of ads:

  - Banner ads, shown while you are playing a level.
  - Rewarded ads, which you may choose to watch in order to unlock a
    level. Watching is always optional.

To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob and its partners may collect and
use certain information from your device, which may include:

  - Advertising ID (such as the Google Advertising ID on Android or the
    IDFA on iOS)
  - IP address and approximate location derived from it
  - Device information (model, operating system version, language,
    screen size, battery and network status)
  - App information and how you interact with ads (such as ad
    impressions, clicks, and whether a rewarded ad was completed)
  - Diagnostic and crash information related to the ad SDK

This information is collected by Google directly, under Google's own
privacy practices, not by us. We do not receive your Advertising ID or
any other personal data from these services.

You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google's Privacy Policy is available here:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOW ADVERTISING DATA IS USED
------------------------------------------------------------

Advertising data is used by Google and its partners to:

  - Deliver ads inside the Game
  - Show ads that may be personalized to your interests, where you have
    allowed this
  - Measure ad performance and prevent fraud and abuse
  - Credit us for rewarded ads that you complete

------------------------------------------------------------
5. YOUR CHOICES AND CONTROLS
------------------------------------------------------------

Consent (EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland):
If you are located in one of these regions, the Game may show a consent
message the first time you open it, in line with applicable law. You can
accept or decline personalized advertising there, and you can change
your choice later where the Game or your device provides that option.

Personalized ads on your device:
  - Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads) lets
    you opt out of ads personalization or reset your Advertising ID.
  - iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking lets you control
    whether apps may ask to track you, and Settings > Privacy & Security
    > Apple Advertising lets you control personalized ads.

If you opt out of personalized ads, you will still see ads, but they
will be less relevant to your interests.

Rewarded ads:
Watching a rewarded ad is entirely optional. The Game remains playable
without watching any ads, by completing levels in order.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
------------------------------------------------------------

The Game is intended for a general audience that may include children.
For users who are children, or who are under the age of consent in their
region, we configure advertising so that it is treated as child-directed
where required by law (for example under COPPA and the GDPR), which
means ads are not personalized and certain data collection is
restricted. If you believe a child has provided personal information
through the Game, please contact us at rhynstudio@gmail.com.

------------------------------------------------------------
7. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION
------------------------------------------------------------

Your game progress is stored only on your device and remains there until
you uninstall the Game or clear its data. We cannot access or delete it
remotely.

Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's own
policies. To request access to, or deletion of, data Google holds about
you, please use the controls in your Google account at
https://myaccount.google.com or follow the instructions in Google's
Privacy Policy.

------------------------------------------------------------
8. DATA SECURITY
------------------------------------------------------------

Because we do not collect or store your personal information on our own
servers, the main data we handle (your game progress) stays on your
device. Please be aware that no method of storage or transmission is
completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
